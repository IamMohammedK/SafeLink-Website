How look-alike links fool you

Read the whole domain, not just the first word. These are the tricks SafeLink flags.

OFFICIAL LOOK-ALIKE THE TRICK
paypal.com paypa1.com A number 1 replaces the letter l.
microsoft.com rnicrosoft.com The letters r and n together imitate m.
alrajhibank.com.sa alrajhibank-secure.com Extra words and hyphens added to a real name.
apple.com apple.com.id-verify.top The real name sits in the subdomain. The actual site is id-verify.top.
